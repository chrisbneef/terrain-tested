# Terrain Tested Synthetic Lubricants: BrandScript

Built from the StoryBrand questionnaire (`terrain-tested-synthetic-lubricants.csv`).
Everything below is drawn from the dealer's own answers. Nothing about his
background, customers or results has been added.

## One-liner

Your machine works harder than the oil aisle knows. Terrain Tested is an
independent AMSOIL Dealer in Eugene, Oregon, run by an operator, mechanic and
racer who will tell you what it needs and why.

## The one thing to remember

This dealer has lived it. He has run, repaired and raced machines like yours
and has hit the same problems you have.

## 1. The hero (the customer)

People whose machines matter to them, for a living or for the love of it:

- **Powersports** performance shops and riders
- **Heavy equipment** owners and operators in construction and industry
- **Small engine** repair and sales shops

**What they want:** the best value, reliability, long life and performance from
their machines. Operators want less downtime. Racers want everything the
machine has. Shops want customers who buy premium and come back.

## 2. The problem

- **External:** Heat, RPM, load and long hours break down ordinary oil. That
  leads to downtime, repairs and failures.
- **Internal:** They are guessing. Nobody at a big-box store has run their
  machine or can say what it needs.
- **Philosophical:** A machine you depend on deserves better than a guess.

## 3. The guide (the dealer)

- **Empathy:** "I've run them, wrenched on them and raced them."
- **Authority:**
  - More than a decade as a heavy equipment operator
  - Mechanic on most every type of machine
  - Certified in marine engines
  - Years in off-road, motocross and drag racing, plus street bikes
  - Independent dealer for AMSOIL, which formulates, blends and packages in the USA

## 4. The plan

1. **Tell me what you run.** Machine, hours, conditions, past problems.
2. **Get a straight recommendation.** The right product for the spec and the use, with the reason.
3. **Run it with confidence.** Order through AMSOIL, with a real person to call.

## 5. Calls to action

- **Direct:** Talk to the dealer (call, text or email)
- **Transitional:** Read the field notes (oil education)

## 6. What failure looks like

They settle for what they know and miss products that would have kept the
machine alive longer or running at its best.

## 7. What success looks like

- They see the value: the higher price up front mostly disappears with longer service intervals.
- They take pride in looking after the machine properly.
- They look at the machine with more appreciation for what it does for them.

## Voice

"Like a screaming bald eagle: American, tested and enduring."

- Plain, direct, confident. Short sentences. First person.
- Talks like someone on a job site or in the pits, not like a brochure.
- Honest about limits: says when the cheaper oil is the right call.
- No hype words, no invented statistics, no claims the dealer cannot back.

## Messaging rules (from AMSOIL dealer policy)

- Always "Independent AMSOIL Dealer". Never imply the site is AMSOIL INC.
- No prices anywhere on the site. Pricing and ordering go through AMSOIL.
- State the real location only: Eugene, Oregon.
- Never name other AMSOIL Dealers or their dealerships.
- These rules also apply to page titles, meta descriptions and structured data.
