# Terrain Tested: design system

## The idea

The site is a USGS topographic quadrangle. The business is called Terrain
Tested and its customers work and ride on real ground, so the site borrows the
grammar of the map they would carry: paper, woodland tint, contour lines,
water, red section lines, a title block and a margin collar.

It reads as American and enduring without flags, eagles, carbon fibre or a
dark "performance" theme, which is what every other oil dealer site uses.

Light theme on purpose: the reader is in a shop, a truck cab or outdoors, in
daylight, often on a phone.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--paper` | `#f6f6ef` | Page ground |
| `--ink` | `#1b1c18` | Text, rules, borders |
| `--ink-soft` | `#4a4c43` | Secondary text |
| `--woodland` | `#cfe2b0` | Full-width regions, answer blocks |
| `--woodland-deep` | `#2f4a22` | Text and rules on woodland |
| `--contour` | `#a9682f` | Contour lines, step numerals |
| `--contour-ink` | `#7a4416` | Small contour-coloured text |
| `--water-ink` | `#145a8a` | Links, focus rings |
| `--section` | `#b3232a` | Primary button, closing band |
| `--section-deep` | `#8e1a20` | Hover, red text on paper |

Colour owns whole regions (woodland sections, the red closing band). It is not
sprinkled as accents.

## Type

- **Archivo Variable** (width axis) for headings, at 112 to 120% width and
  800 to 900 weight. Uppercase only for the hero, legend names and the closing call.
- **Public Sans Variable** for body text. It is the U.S. government's typeface,
  which suits a government-map world.
- Both are self-hosted through Fontsource. No external font requests.
- Body measure is capped at 68 characters.

## Components

- **Title block** (home hero): bordered panel with a double neatline, sitting on the full-bleed map.
- **Strip**: interior page heading on woodland, with the map surfacing on the right.
- **Legend**: the three applications as ruled rows, not cards.
- **Record / conditions**: ruled definition lists for facts.
- **Plan**: three numbered steps. Numbered because the order matters.
- **Answer block**: the short answer at the top of every field note.
- **Close**: red closing band with the single call to action.
- **Collar**: the footer, with a scale bar and the dealer disclaimer.

## Rules

- No labels stacked above headings. No icon-card grids. No gradient text.
- One motion moment: the hero map unrolls once. It is off under reduced motion.
- Rules and borders are 1px or 2px ink. Corners are square.
- The map (`public/topo.svg`) is generated by `npm run topo`. It is invented
  terrain, not a map of a real place.
